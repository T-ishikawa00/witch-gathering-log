## Full 90-day progression reruns

Two fresh 90-day, file-backed actual-command runs used the unchanged R025 harness with R014 enabled and growth250BP. Each day reopened the persisted snapshot into a new repository and checked equality. No paid/ad grants were added.

The 250BP passive result has all five regions unlocked, so the blocker is combat capability against the last two bosses rather than an inaccessible region gate. The harness continues attempting those bosses daily, which accounts for its 72 losses; these are repeated failed commands/combats, not 72 distinct enemies. No challenge attempts occur after the boss progression failure. The free_ward 250BP run remains viable despite a lower growth multiplier; this points to earned investment/loadout policy as a key confounder in any growth-only claim.

## Paired saved-checkpoint combat sensitivity

The same day30/day90 saves were also read-only hashed and cloned for actual `.equip` and `.resolveEncounter(B_R05_B, seed: 100049)` command dispatch at both 250BP and 500BP. This produced 48 rows; all available pairs won. For active legal loadouts, 500BP shortened this single boss by 1.1–3.2s. For the passive Q0 starter set, it shortened the boss by 46.4s and increased total survivor HP from 20 to 132. Full per-actor and gear-instance values are in [LOADOUT_MATRIX_R002_GROWTH_SENSITIVITY.json](loadout_checks/LOADOUT_MATRIX_R002_GROWTH_SENSITIVITY.json). The active-loadout effects are smaller, and one seed/one boss cannot establish balance.

At day30 and day90, the active saves each had 45 owned gear instances and 24 equipped slots; the passive save had 5 instances and 5 equipped slots. The passive checkpoint inventory contains exactly five instances: EQ_W_01, EQ_A_01, EQ_S_01, EQ_F_01, and EQ_BODY_01. The four weapons are the canonical starters; EQ_BODY_01 is the one crafted item. No other item is present in that saved inventory. Alternative four-weapon build variants were unavailable there and were recorded as unavailable, not synthesized. Both active snapshots had the three requested alternative builds available; the fourth selected weapon composition was the saved loadout. This difference is a real inventory/policy confound, not a paired inventory-controlled growth experiment.
