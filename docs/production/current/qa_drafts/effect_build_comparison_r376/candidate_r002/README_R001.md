# R377 owned-effect previews — source only

This additive test defines 24 no-reward previews from four pairs of gear UUIDs already present in the archived R368 free/no-ad simulation save. Pairs compare barrier, Smith counter, Apprentice heal, and Familiar chase effects in authored compatible challenges, over three fixed seeds. Each pair changes one UUID in one actor slot; all other occupied slots, party, runtime, challenge, and seed are held fixed.

All comparison items are same quality/enhancement/refinement/investment within their pair. Natural latent slots differ; full slot rolls and event traces are retained, so results remain observational and do not isolate pure causal value. The test requires each listed candidate event family to occur at least once over its three candidate previews. Failure means not observed in this bounded setup, not permission to weaken the check.

R368 is a virtual cohort, not ordinary human play. No grant, material, XP, ticket, currency, or save write is made. This does not establish optimality, acquisition accessibility, balance, fun, paid value, formal adoption, or completion of the 15 design research goals. This source is not compiled or run.
